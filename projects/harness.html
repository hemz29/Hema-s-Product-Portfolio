I built my own AI workbench to see what the models see

Harness puts Claude and Gemini side by side, grounds them in my documents, emails and live web search, and shows exactly what context each answer was built from.

Why build one

As a product person working on RAG systems, I wanted to feel the trade-offs rather than read about them. What happens to an answer when a document gets truncated? When search is on or off? When the same question goes to two different models?

What it does

  • Two models, one thread. Ask Claude or Gemini, or compare their answers to the same prompt.
  • Grounded on demand. Attach a document, paste an email thread to draft a reply, or switch on live web search.
  • Nothing hidden. A context inspector shows every piece of attached context, its size in tokens and whether it was truncated. Each reply shows the model, latency and estimated tokens.
  • My keys, my machine. API keys and chat history stay in the browser.
If you can’t see the context, you can’t trust the answer.

What’s next

Connecting it to the apps I use every day (calendar, mail and notes) so it becomes a true personal assistant, plus more model adapters and voice input.
