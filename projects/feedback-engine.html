Listening to an engineering platform’s users at scale

As product owner for two capabilities of an internal engineering platform, I needed the voice of the customer at the centre of every decision, not just the loudest voice in the room. So I built an automated feedback heatmap.

The problem

Platform feedback arrives everywhere: tickets, chats, interviews and surveys. Read one by one, it is noise. Prioritised by whoever shouts loudest, it is politics.

What I built

  • An automated feedback heatmap that brings user feedback together and shows where the demand clusters, feeding feature discovery and prioritisation.
  • User interviews to explain the why behind the heat.
  • Figma mock-ups to test ideas with users early and give engineers a clear target.
  • Feature and story breakdowns with tech leads, for capacity planning and predictable delivery.
Prioritise by signal, not by volume.
