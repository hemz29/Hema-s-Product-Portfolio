When the homework is in a language the parents can’t read

Many Bengaluru children study Kannada as a third language, and most of their parents can’t check a single answer. Kali Kannada takes a child from the school chapter to a marked, handwritten exam paper.

The problem

I’m one of these parents. When my child sits down to revise for a Kannada test, I can’t tell whether an answer is right. Of the 17 parents I surveyed, 14 said the same. They didn’t lack content: they had textbooks, tutors, translators and ChatGPT. What they lacked was confidence. They needed help understanding the chapter, practice in the format the school actually tests, and an honest check of written answers.

The insight

Generic AI can explain a word. It can’t tell you whether your child’s handwritten answer would score marks on a school paper. So I built around that workflow, not around chat.

The strongest pain was confidence, not content.

The decisions

  • Built for paper, not screens. Printable school-format worksheets with a one-hour timer, answered by hand and uploaded with a phone photo.
  • The child confirms what the machine read. Before anything is marked, the student checks the reading of their handwriting, so a misread never costs marks. No competitor I reviewed does this.
  • Parents’ doubts shaped the product. “I don’t want to give my Google login” became guest access. “Something to help me trust this” became a founder story and an educator content review.
  • Say what isn’t proven. The roadmap is kept apart from what’s live, and licensing comes before any wider launch.

Early signals

In an invite-only pilot with families from one school, 7 children made 24 visits averaging 12 minutes, and parents printed 11 worksheets. Two of the three parents who gave feedback said they would use it for the next test. What it doesn’t prove yet: retention, better results, or willingness to pay. That is the next milestone.

What’s next

Speaking practice, parent-uploaded content, more chapters and grades, then a school partnership before any subscription.
